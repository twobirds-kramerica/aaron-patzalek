## 1-Page (Full Bio)

Aaron Patzalek is a product leader and solopreneur based in St. Thomas, Ontario. Over 20+ years, he led digital product delivery across telecom (TELUS, Start.ca), retail (Staples Canada), eCommerce (Goodwill, Peavey Industries), and non-profit — a career defined by being brought in to fix broken systems, build new ones, and ship.

In early 2026, Aaron founded Two Birds Innovation. The name comes from his twin daughters — fraternal twins, both girls. Everything he builds is, at its core, for them and for people like them who deserve tools that respect their intelligence and their time.

In the first 90 days, Aaron shipped 7 digital products as a solo founder with no external funding:

- **Digital Confidence Centre** — a free, bilingual digital literacy programme for Canadian seniors. 29 self-paced modules covering online safety, banking, video calls, AI, and more. Built with anxiety-first design: Module 1 teaches the escape hatch before anything else. Every interface decision tested against Brenda, a real user persona representing a 74-year-old alone with her iPad. Now available coast to coast.

- **HAL Stack** — a voice-first AI infrastructure with a four-layer sovereignty model, 31-persona company boardroom simulation, automated sprint system, and cross-context knowledge bridge. Built entirely in markdown, JSON, and vanilla JavaScript. Zero npm dependencies.

- **Clarity** — an AI-powered business diagnostic for small and medium enterprises.

- **Career Coach** — an AI job application tool that analyses postings, scores fit, and prepares candidates for interviews.

Aaron's approach sits at the intersection of two frameworks: Essentialism (strip away 60% of operational noise) and Loveability (design what remains for deep human resonance). His sovereignty principle — your data stays yours, your decisions stay yours — runs through every product. He doesn't build tools that act for users; he builds tools that empower users to act for themselves.

Before founding Two Birds, Aaron spent 5+ years at TELUS and Koodo, where he led product teams shipping software used by millions. At Staples Canada, he managed web-to-print platform delivery and contributed to process improvement as a Six Sigma SME. At Start.ca, Goodwill, and Peavey, he brought the same pattern: arrive, map the system, strip the waste, build what matters, ship.

Aaron holds a diploma from Fanshawe College and a Certified Scrum Master designation. He's an INTJ systems thinker, a solo parent, and someone who believes the best technology amplifies human judgment rather than replacing it.

He is building towards CA$10,000/month in revenue by September 2026 — through library partnerships, SME consulting, and digital products that prove their worth by the outcomes they create.
