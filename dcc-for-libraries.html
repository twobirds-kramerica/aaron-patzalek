What it is

Free for patrons. Ready to run.

📚

29 modules. No login required.

Patrons open a browser, click a module, and learn. No account, no app, no password to forget. Fully functional over any connection, on any device.

📝

Plain English. Big text. Real situations.

Written for adults who are new to digital. Every module uses real examples, simple language, and large text. Designed for seniors and newcomers first, accessible to everyone.

📊

Measurable outcomes. NHSP-compatible.

Each module tracks completion locally. You can pull session counts to demonstrate outcomes to NHSP funders, BDS reporting, or your board, without collecting patron data.
