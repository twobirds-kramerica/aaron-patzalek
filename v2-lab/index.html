How I work

The honest-operator rules

  • Find the real problem first. Every engagement starts with framing, not tooling. Most "AI problems" turn out to be process problems wearing a costume.
  • No hype, ever. If a tool is not worth adopting, I will say so, even when saying so ends the project. That is the point of hiring an independent evaluator.
  • Bring the people along. Adoption fails on trust, not technology. 15+ years of leading teams taught me that nobody transforms an organization from a slide deck.
  • Ship for the end user. The measure of the work is whether it genuinely helps the person at the end of the chain. Everything else is overhead.
