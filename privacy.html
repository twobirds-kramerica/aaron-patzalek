Privacy Policy

Aaron Patzalek · Two Birds Innovation · Last updated: June 2026

Plain language summary: This site collects your contact details only if you fill in and submit the contact form or book a consulting call. Contact form data goes to Formspree for delivery. Booking data goes to Calendly. There are no analytics, no tracking, and no cookies.

Contact Form

When you use the contact form on this site, the following information is submitted to Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form delivery service:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The message you typed

This data is delivered to me via email. Formspree may store your submission temporarily to facilitate delivery. Review Formspree's privacy policy for details on their data handling.

I use the information you submit only to respond to your inquiry. I do not add you to any mailing list or share your contact details.

Booking Calls

If you book a consulting call using the scheduling links on the consulting page, your booking request is processed by Calendly (calendly.com), a third-party scheduling service. The following information is collected by Calendly at the time of booking:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your selected meeting time

This data is used solely to schedule and confirm the meeting. Review Calendly's privacy notice for details on their data handling. I use the information to prepare for and conduct the meeting only.

Analytics and Tracking

This site does not use Google Analytics, cookies, or any tracking service. No data about your visit is collected or sent anywhere.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies.

Your Rights Under PIPEDA

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of any personal information I hold about you. If you have submitted a contact form and would like your information removed, email me and I will delete it promptly.
