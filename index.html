About

I spent two decades leading product teams at TELUS, Staples, Start.ca, and Goodwill — shipping software that reached millions of Canadians. At every company, the pattern was the same: arrive, map the system, strip the waste, build what matters, ship.

In early 2026, I founded Two Birds Innovation. The name comes from my twin daughters — fraternal twins, both girls. Everything I build is, at its core, for them and for people like them who deserve tools that respect their intelligence and their time.

I believe the best technology amplifies human judgment rather than replacing it. My products follow a simple rule: the user drives, I provide the engine.

How I Work

Four principles that guide everything I build.

Essentialism

Strip away 60% of the noise. Do fewer things, but do them well enough that people love them. Say no to good so you can say yes to great.

Sovereignty

Your data stays yours. Your decisions stay yours. Every product I build works at four layers — from cloud to fully offline. No vendor lock-in. No surprise paywalls.

Lead from the Back

I provide the engine and the vantage point. You sit at the steering wheel and drive your own progress. I don't do things for people — I empower people to do things for themselves.

Ship and Iterate

Perfect is the enemy of done. Build the minimum lovable thing, get it in front of real people, and improve based on what you learn. 7 products in 90 days, not 1 product in 2 years.
