The Story Behind Two Birds

The name comes from my twin daughters, fraternal twins, both girls. In British and Canadian slang, "birds" has historically referred to girls. The name is personal and deliberate: everything I build is, at its core, for them.

But the name carries a deeper meaning. Fraternal twins start at the exact same point yet grow into uniquely different people. That's the philosophy behind every product: ideas, growth, and people must be empowered to travel in their own sovereign directions. Not corralled. Not automated. Sovereign.

The Chevrons ><

The Two Birds logo is two offset chevrons (angle brackets from code) pointing in opposite directions. They represent:

  • Navigational forces: the intersection of headwinds (the challenge of change and displacement) and tailwinds (the momentum of AI)
  • The meeting point: where the "Honest Helper" (the consultancy) meets the "Brave User"
  • Fraternal paths: related but distinct, like the twins who inspired the name
  • Code: the angle bracket is the most fundamental symbol in programming

Sovereignty Over Autonomy

Most companies in this space build tools that act for the user: replacing human effort, making decisions on your behalf, creating dependency. I build tools that empower the user to act for themselves. The user retains ownership of their data, their logic, and their destiny. That's not a feature. It's a design philosophy.

The Framework

Every decision passes through two lenses: Essentialism (strip away 60% of the noise, do fewer things better) and Lovability (design what remains for deep human resonance: build things people love, not just use).

How I Work

Essentialism

Strip away 60% of the noise. Do fewer things, but do them well enough that people love them. Say no to good so you can say yes to great.

Sovereignty

Your data stays yours. Your decisions stay yours. Every product works at four layers,from cloud to fully offline. No vendor lock-in. No surprise paywalls.

Lead from the Back

I provide the engine and the vantage point. You sit at the steering wheel and drive your own progress. I don't do things for people,I empower people to do things for themselves.

Ship and Iterate

Perfect is the enemy of done. Build the minimum lovable thing, get it in front of real people, and improve based on what you learn.
