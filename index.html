The Short Version

Product leader. Solo parent. Builder of things that matter.

I spent two decades managing product at companies like Telus, leading teams that shipped software used by millions. I learned what works, what doesn't, and — more importantly — what actually helps people.

In early 2026, I founded Two Birds Innovation. The name comes from my twin daughters. Everything I build is, at its core, for them — and for people like them who deserve tools that respect their intelligence and their time.

I believe the best technology doesn't replace human judgment — it amplifies it. My products follow a simple rule: the user drives, we provide the engine.

How I Work

Four principles that guide everything I build.

Essentialism

Strip away 60% of the noise. Do fewer things, but do them well enough that people love them.

Sovereignty

Your data stays yours. Your decisions stay yours. The tools I build empower you — they never replace you.

Lead from the Back

I provide the engine and the vantage point. You sit at the steering wheel and drive your own progress.

Ship and Iterate

Perfect is the enemy of done. Build the minimum lovable thing, get it in front of real people, and improve from there.
