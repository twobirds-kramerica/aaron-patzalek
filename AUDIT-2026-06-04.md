## Anti-Patterns Verdict: FAIL

The site's own stated brand voice says "No em dashes." They appear in the hero, philosophy banner, story section, and career timeline. PRODUCT.md anti-references specifically name "guru/coach sites (Tony Robbins aesthetic, motivational language)." The current philosophy banner reads:

> "Always forward. Never quit. Grow bravely. Support with care."
> "Be YOUR best self, not someone else's."

That is motivational language. It immediately undermines the "senior colleague, direct, no corporate filler" brand personality.

## Detailed Findings

### [P0] Em dashes throughout — violates stated brand voice
- **Locations:** Hero (`I'm Aaron Patzalek &mdash;`), products subtitle, story section (x4), career timeline (x7+)
- **Fix:** Global replace `&mdash;` with commas, colons, or restructured sentences. No exceptions.
- **Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P0] Philosophy banner = motivational language anti-pattern
- **Location:** `<section class="section section--dark philosophy-banner">` — immediately after hero
- **Impact:** "Be YOUR best self" is word-for-word the pattern the anti-references forbid. Categorises Aaron alongside life coaches before he's had a chance to establish credibility.
- **Fix:** Remove entirely. If something must replace it, use one specific evidenced claim: "I have shipped software used by millions of Canadians."
- **Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P0] Identical card grid — 4 products, same structure
- **Location:** `.card-grid` — KevsCasa, HAL Stack, Clarity, Career Coach
- **Impact:** Impeccable absolute ban. Emoji icons (lightbulb, briefcase, house, gear) are generic and decorative.
- **Fix:** Differentiate layouts. HAL Stack in particular needs different treatment — it's infrastructure, not a product.
- **Command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] Hero copy repeated verbatim in products subtitle
- Hero: "Building tools that empower people to act for themselves."
- Products section: "I build tools that empower the user to act for themselves."
- **Fix:** Products subtitle should introduce the products, not re-state the hero. E.g., "Four tools. All free. All Canadian."
- **Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P1] Dual audience — no clear path split
- **Impact:** Consulting clients and employers land on the same hero with the same CTAs. PRODUCT.md principle 2: "One entry point per audience."
- **Fix:** Split hero CTAs to route each audience to their relevant content. Structural change — needs shape approval.
- **Command:** `/impeccable shape` (Aaron approval required Thursday June 5)

### [P1] Section order doesn't follow earn-trust sequence
- **Current:** Hero → Philosophy Banner → Products → Story → Career
- **PRODUCT.md principle 3:** Problem → credentials → offer → action
- **Fix:** Move career/credentials before product showcase for both audiences. Remove philosophy banner. Story can come last.
- **Command:** `/impeccable shape` (Aaron approval required)

### [P1] Stale stat: "26 DCC modules" — should be 29
- **Location:** `.about-stats` sidebar
- **Fix:** Change 26 to 29. One edit, 30 seconds.

### [P2] Inter font — no display heading pairing
- **Location:** `css/tokens.css` — `--font-sans: 'Inter'` used for everything
- **Fix:** Add DM Serif Display (already used on Career Coach) for h1/h2 headings.
- **Command:** `/impeccable typeset`
