Operations first. The products come out of the shop floor.

I set out to build a product and found the real problem was the factory behind it: how one person builds, tests, ships and adapts software without a development team.

So I built the shop floor first: the guardrails that keep it safe, the governance that keeps it honest, and the process flexibility that lets a business person run real software projects at near-zero cost. AI agents do the building. I set the direction, make the decisions and check the work. Five tools and learning sites are publicly viewable. None has launched; all are in alpha or early beta. The concepts and internal work below show where the next push could go.

Featured concept · In design · Not built as a product yet

A guided toolkit for starting your own fractional, consulting or side-gig business [working name: none yet]

A Wix-style guided builder for new independent businesses in Southwestern Ontario. Say what you want to build: a consultancy, store, product or fractional role, plus your reach and goals. The guide would walk you through:

  1. Direction: a business plan and a practical roadmap.
  2. Structure: sole proprietorship, corporation or partnership, with plain-language risks and rewards.
  3. Registration: registering the business and sales tax (HST) basics, pointing to official sources.
  4. Checkpoints: insurance, legal considerations and certifications to consider, with official sources and professional advice where needed.
  5. Practical setup: calendar and booking, business email, a simple website, and an offer and price page.
  6. AI help at each step: using the person's own AI models.

For: new fractional and side-gig workers, students starting a business, and municipal and community economic-development teams offering a tool to local entrepreneurs.

Potential: promising to test locally; the guided product remains a concept.
